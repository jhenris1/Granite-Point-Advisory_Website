About

Hi, I'm Jason

I started Granite Point Advisory after spending nearly a decade helping businesses make important financial decisions through investment banking, corporate development, financial planning, and strategic finance roles.

Throughout my career, I saw the same problem repeatedly: business owners were working incredibly hard but often lacked clear, reliable financial information to guide decisions. Without accurate books and meaningful reporting, even great businesses can end up operating on instinct rather than insight.

Today, I work directly with business owners to keep their finances organized, provide clear reporting, and help them better understand the numbers behind their business. My goal is simple: give owners the financial clarity they need to make confident decisions and focus on growth.

When I'm not working with clients, you'll usually find me cycling, spending time with family, or preparing for the arrival of my first child.
